# Methodology Reference

This file summarizes the methodological logic encoded in the skills. It is a working guide, not a substitute for reading and citing original research.

## 1. Cultural public sphere and #JogjaOraDidol
Fahmi (2019) studies the #JogjaOraDidol conversation using a mixed method combining topic modelling, social network analysis (SNA), and discourse analysis. The paper treats digital interaction as affective cultural expression and discusses structural, representational, and interactional dimensions of the public sphere. It relates platform discourse to policy agenda-setting and evaluation while requiring attention to whether online expression connects to real action.

Operational lesson: combine macro-level patterns (topics and networks) with contextual close reading and policy chronology. A hashtag corpus does not represent all residents or all affected stakeholders.

## 2. Principal-agent relations in social media
Fahmi, Wibowo, and Yudanto (2018) examine interactions between a government account (@KemenDesa) and citizens mentioning it, using tweets from 1 October 2014 through 31 September 2017 as stated in the article abstract. The study frames government-society interaction as a principal-agent relationship and uses the interaction process and content to assess communication.

Operational lesson: map roles, mandates, inbound requests, replies, responsiveness, and accountability. Avoid treating reply volume as service resolution or an official account as proof of institutional mandate without corroboration.

## 3. Hybrid text mining and qualitative analysis
Fahmi, Wibowo, and Yudanto (2018) argue that text mining can process unstructured social media data but cannot fully interpret textual meaning. A hybrid approach uses computational methods to identify patterns and qualitative analysis to interpret the contexts and meanings behind them.

Operational lesson: use computational analysis for scale and qualitative analysis for context. Build transparent preprocessing, sampling, coding, validation, and integration steps.

## 4. Method integration protocol
1. Define constructs and research questions before selecting algorithms.
2. Document data provenance, population boundary, unit of analysis, sampling, and missingness.
3. Preserve raw text; version normalized data and code.
4. Use models to create hypotheses and samples for inspection, not as final evidence.
5. Validate on human-reviewed examples; report errors and uncertainty.
6. Integrate pattern-level and case-level findings in a joint display.
7. Triangulate with primary documents, interviews, field evidence, and institutional records when available.
8. Separate description, interpretation, and causal claims.
